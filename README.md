 # Mini Feed Simulator

Welcome to your tiny corner of the internet! **Mini Feed Simulator** is a friendly, no-login social feed where you can create posts, discover users, sort the conversation, and give posts a little love.

## Start Exploring

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `Scripts/index.html` in your browser.
3. Say hello by creating your first post.

No server, database, or account is needed. Everything happens right in your browser.

## What You Can Do

- Create posts with a username and message.
- Like posts as many times as you want.
- Sort the feed by **Newest**, **Oldest**, or **Likes**.
- Search for posts from a specific user.
- Delete posts whenever you change your mind.
- Enjoy a handful of sample posts loaded into the feed for a quick start.

## A Fun First Run

Try this mini challenge:

1. Search for `David` or `Jade`.
2. Like your favorite post a few times.
3. Switch to **Likes** and watch the feed reshuffle.
4. Clear the search, then add a post of your own.
